# Graphics G7B - Coordinated cinematic daylight

The shared daylight response now combines stronger, restrained warm direct sunlight with a cool hemispherical sky fill and softer ground bounce. Vertical facades receive clearer directional contrast. A continuous, hue-preserving brightness shoulder protects pale roofs and stone without flattening ordinary facade illumination; metal highlights remain restrained. Material albedos, facade patterns, glass Fresnel/reflection weights, transparency and water animation are preserved.

`DayAtmosphere::sunRayDirection` is the single CPU source: **(-0.72, -1.0, -0.432)**. It gives approximately **49.98 degrees elevation**, retaining the previous sun azimuth. Scene uniforms and the existing shadow projection use it directly; the sky shader's normalized sun direction is generated from that same constant. The G7A gradient, haze, cloud coordinates, density and shape code are unchanged. Sky glow and cloud illumination follow the coordinated sun.

Residential near-fragment and medium/far vertex-lit glass use the same daylight function. Abu Dhabi, Talan, Khan tower, Nur Alem and greenhouse glazing retain their specialized reflection/tint designs while using shared diffuse illumination. Northern Lights retains its turquoise coating and facade pattern with less flat local fill. Astana Arena's satin shell uses a more directional light balance; its model and materials are unchanged. Existing sheltered seating/interior fill remains separate from the exterior balance.

Debug x64 and full-scene shader/link/OpenGL checks passed. Five matched before/after views were inspected: residential street, elevated city overview, Baiterek, Astana Arena and Northern Lights. The street and stadium show clearly stronger form/contrast, while the blue-green glazing and pale stone remain readable. GL framebuffer, viewport, texture bindings, depth/blend/offset state, F6 press/hold/focus handling, sun uniform agreement and stationary shadow-map reuse passed.

No new geometry, textures, visible-scene draw calls, rendering passes or shadow maps. GPU instancing, LOD, culling, glass caches and G6 filtering/bias/update logic are untouched. The changed sun direction alters the existing light-frustum's caster selection: the matched overview refresh submitted **58,164 depth triangles / 27 depth draws**, versus **50,736 / 22** previously; these occur when the cached map refreshes, not every stationary frame. Resolution remains 2048 square. A short stationary Debug 1280x720 F6-ON overview sample measured **94.4 FPS / 10.59 ms** on Intel UHD. The initial baseline timing was inconsistent, so no reliable performance delta or speedup is claimed.

Changed: `src/SkyAtmosphere.cpp/.h`, `src/Scene.h`, `src/BuildingMaterials.h`, `src/BackgroundResidential.cpp`, `shaders/ground.frag`, and this document. No layout, geometry, camera/navigation, material pattern or unrelated source changes. Daylight remains an inexpensive display-referred approximation, not a physical sky/GI system.
